Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2024

1. Introduction

Beijing Dayue Shengqi Technology Co., Ltd. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website dayueshengqi.com or use our mobile applications available on Google Play and Apple App Store.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out contact forms on our website
  • Subscribe to our newsletters
  • Register for accounts
  • Use our mobile applications
  • Contact us via email or phone

This information may include: name, email address, phone number, mailing address, company name, job title, and any other information you provide.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website or use our applications, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and identifiers
  • Operating system
  • Referring/exit pages
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages viewed and actions taken

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Respond to your inquiries and fulfill your requests
  • Send you technical notices, updates, and support information
  • Send you marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

Our mobile management applications may collect:

4.3 Advertising Types

Our mobile applications may display the following types of advertisements:

  • Splash Ads: Full-screen advertisements displayed when the app launches
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Video advertisements that users can watch in exchange for in-app rewards
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen advertisements displayed at natural transition points
  • Banner Ads: Rectangular advertisements displayed at the top or bottom of the screen
  • Native Ads: Advertisements that match the form and function of the app interface
